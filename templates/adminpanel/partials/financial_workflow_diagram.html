How to use the client-to-cost-centre workflow

A visual guide for Financial Admin, Centre Heads and Finance.

No placeholder cost centres
  1. 1Lead

    Add the client opportunity

    Capture the client, contact person and bid or opportunity title. The Financial Admin becomes its owner.

  2. 2Engage

    Log every engagement

    Record calls, meetings, minutes and follow-up dates against the client opportunity—not a cost centre.

  3. 3Bid

    Prepare and submit

    Track the bid reference, deadline, application date and estimated value. The estimate is not treated as income.

  4. 4Decision

    Record the outcome

    Update the pipeline when the client responds.

    Lost → closeWon → continue
  5. 5Pending

    Wait for official details

    The won bid remains “cost centre pending” until UJ issues the official cost-centre name and code.

  6. 6Activate

    Create the real cost centre

    Enter the official name and code. All earlier client engagement notes link automatically.

  7. 7Finance

    Confirm contract and monitor

    Add the actual contract amount when received, then manage payments, budgets and expenditure in Finance.

Core rule: Client history starts immediately. A cost centre begins only after a win and official UJ allocation.
